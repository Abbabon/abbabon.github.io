Card Kings: Magical Journey - a social-casual mobile game where you draw a
hand of cards instead of spinning a slot: coin hands pay out, bow hands send
you to a balloon-shooting loot drop, and the right suit lets you raid a
sleeping dragon's hoard. The first Jelly Button project built on DI and a
domain-based architecture from day one. Almost launched; cryo-frozen in a
single day when the team was moved to Merge Stories.
