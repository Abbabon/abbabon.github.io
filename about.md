I'm **Amit Netanel** — a game developer and software engineer from Jerusalem. I build
games, and more often, I build the tools, pipelines and architecture that other
people build games with. Unity and C# are my daily drivers; the mess underneath
them is my actual specialty.

Twenty years in, roughly: eight of them in games, the rest in backends, fullstack,
mobile and DevOps. I've been a developer, a tech lead, a lecturer, a podcaster and
a one-man consultancy — sometimes several of those in the same week.

## The things that don't fit in a CV

* I'm programming-language and ecosystem agnostic. Web-scraping bots in Python,
  games in Unity, fullstack in Angular/Rails, photo processing in Matlab,
  mainframe migrations in C#. I've seen some s$#@!

* What actually gets me out of bed: tools for level design, shader programming,
  3D software rendering, procedural generation, mobile performance optimization,
  [constraint-based game programming](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php), and
  build pipelines that don't ruin anyone's afternoon.

* I care loudly about clean code and craftsmanship. Three years of podcasting
  about it with Yura will do that to a person.

* I currently own 7 mechanical keyboards. I wonder if my 1986 Model M qualifies;
  if it does then I own 8.

* In my limited spare time I learn Japanese (via a wicked combination of
  [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com/profile/abbabon),
  [WaniKani](https://www.wanikani.com/users/abbabon),
  [KaniWani](https://kaniwani.com/),
  [Anki Flashcards of Genki I && II](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/942922371) and
  [Tandem](https://www.tandem.net/)). しかし、私はまだ上手していません.

* I run a Dungeons & Dragons campaign, ostensibly to practice writing and game
  design. Mostly it's an excuse to make my friends roll dice.

* Mezookan is Hebrew for 'bearded'. My strength stems from my beard.
