    What it does

    Small app. Fast hands.

    Instant fuzzy search

    Results re-rank as you type. Missing letters are forgiven: lightnig finds Lightning Bolt.

    Inline filters

    Scryfall-style: r:rare t:creature c:r mv>=3 o:"draw a card" s:set. A leading - negates; spaces mean AND.

    Every printing

    Each printing of a card, with set, year and rarity. Hover one to see its art.

    Sets, and what's new

    A sets sidebar, plus NEW and SOON pills on the start screen. A Recently Added column, custom lists and pins keep your own cards close.

    Two endless games

    Guess the Card. Guess the Artist. Three lives, a best score and a streak to beat.

    Offline and private

    Card data comes from Scryfall and lives on your Mac. After the card images have downloaded, it works with no connection. No accounts, no analytics: Data Not Collected.
